# DealFlow360 - Project Overview

DealFlow360 is an enterprise B2B sales and deal-management platform designed to govern the full deal lifecycle:
**Customer → Quotation → Pricing → Discount → Risk Evaluation → Approval → Negotiation → Re-evaluation → Confirmation → Fulfillment (Stock/Backorder) → Billing (Invoice/Payment) → Subscriptions & Deal Health**.

## Core Problem
Enterprise B2B deal negotiation often suffers from disconnected pricing spreadsheets, rogue discounts, unmanaged margin risks, slow manual approvals, and disjointed handoffs to fulfillment and billing.

## Solution
DealFlow360 unifies the sales representative, sales manager, finance team, operations/warehouse, and customer within a single coherent workspace:
- **Central Business Entity**: The Quotation connects every phase of the commercial transaction.
- **Authoritative Business Engines**: Server-side pricing, discount governance, risk scoring, multi-tier approvals, inventory allocation, and billing.
- **Customer Self-Service Portal**: Customers can review quotations, counter-offer or negotiate, and confirm deals with full real-time re-evaluation.
